Welcome to Chembur Social

Chembur SOCIAL is where great food, creative cocktails, work, and good times come together. Located in the heart of Chembur, Mumbai, it brings SOCIAL’s signature blend of a café, bar, and community space to the neighbourhood.

Whether you're catching up with friends, grabbing a quick bite, working over coffee, or unwinding with drinks and music, Chembur SOCIAL offers a lively and relaxed atmosphere for every occasion. With a vibrant setting, delicious food, refreshing beverages, and an energetic vibe, it’s more than just a place to eat — it’s a place to connect, create, and SOCIALize.

Great food, warm hospitality, and memorable moments—that’s what we’re all about.

Fresh Citrus Salad

A vibrant and refreshing salad featuring crisp leafy greens, juicy citrus slices, and creamy cheese, finished with fresh herbs for a bright, wholesome bite.

Loaded Veggie Pizza

A deliciously crispy, cheesy pizza topped with fresh vegetables, herbs, and savory seasonings—perfect for sharing or enjoying on its own.

Raspberry Cream Cake

Soft sponge cake layered with creamy raspberry filling and topped with fresh raspberries. A sweet and fruity delight in every bite.

Mediterranean Buddha Bowl

A colorful and wholesome bowl filled with roasted chickpeas, fresh vegetables, olives, figs, peas, creamy yogurt, and pita bread.
